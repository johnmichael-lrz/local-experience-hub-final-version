Welcome to LIFELINE Makati

LIFELINE Makati is a community-based emergency services hub designed to connect residents with critical resources during disasters, medical emergencies, and crisis situations.

Our mission is to ensure that every Makati resident has immediate access to life-saving information when they need it most. From fire emergencies to mental health crises, we serve as your centralized directory for verified hotlines, trained volunteer responders, and safety information.

  • Verified emergency hotline numbers
  • Dedicated volunteer service providers
  • Interactive survival & safety guides
  • 24/7 community hazard reporting system
