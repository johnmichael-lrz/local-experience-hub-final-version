Welcome to LIFELINE Makati

LIFELINE Makati is a community-based emergency services hub designed to connect residents with critical resources during disasters, medical emergencies, and crisis situations. We serve as a centralized directory for verified hotlines, trained volunteer responders, and safety information.

Our mission is to ensure that every Makati resident has immediate access to life-saving information when they need it most. From fire emergencies to mental health crises, LIFELINE Makati is your one-stop community resource.

  • Verified emergency hotline numbers
  • Trained volunteer service providers
  • Interactive first aid guides
  • 24/7 hazard reporting system

What to Bring: Survival Kit

Pack these essentials before heading to an evacuation center.

Valid ID

Government-issued ID for registration at shelters

Water & Food

At least 3 days' supply of drinking water and non-perishable food

First Aid

Basic first aid kit with bandages, antiseptic, and pain relievers

Documents

Copies of birth certificates, insurance, and property titles

Medications

Prescription medicines and vitamins for at least one week
