Fire Extinguisher Guide

An interactive guide to fire extinguisher parts. Tap or click each quadrant to learn how to use it properly.

Interactive Fire Extinguisher

Tap anywhere on the 6 quadrants of the image to learn the P.A.S.S. method and parts.

SQUEEZE

Squeeze the top handle to discharge the extinguishing agent.

Got it

PULL

Pull the safety pin to break the tamper seal and unlock the handle.

Got it

AIM

Grab the hose and aim it directly at the base of the fire.

Got it

GAUGE

Check the pressure gauge monthly. The needle must be in the green zone.

Got it

NOZZLE

Keep a safe distance. The extinguishing agent shoots out from this nozzle.

Got it

SWEEP

Sweep from side to side at the base of the flames until the fire is out.
