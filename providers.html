Service Providers

Meet the dedicated volunteers and professionals who keep our community safe.

Provider Categories

Our network includes six key categories of emergency service providers.

Paramedics & EMTs

Trained emergency medical technicians providing on-site first response and ambulance services.

  • CPR & AED operation
  • Wound management
  • Patient transport
Firefighters

Frontline fire suppression teams equipped with modern rescue tools and apparatus.

  • Fire suppression
  • Structural rescue
  • Hazmat response
Law Enforcement

Police officers maintaining peace and order, especially during emergency operations.

  • Crowd management
  • Traffic control
  • Security perimeter
Social Workers

Professionals providing psychosocial support, shelter coordination, and family assistance.

  • Crisis counseling
  • Evacuation assistance
  • Relief distribution
Disaster Response Volunteers

Community volunteers trained in search, rescue, and relief operations after disasters.

  • Search & rescue
  • Debris clearing
  • Supply logistics
Community Health Workers

Nurses and health workers providing primary care, immunization, and health education.

  • Basic health screening
  • Immunization drives
  • Health education
