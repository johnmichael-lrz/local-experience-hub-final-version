About LIFELINE Makati

Your trusted community emergency services hub since 2026.

Our Mission

LIFELINE Makati was established to address a critical gap in community preparedness: the lack of a centralized, accessible directory for emergency services. Many residents in Makati City rely on informal social media groups during disasters, which often leads to misinformation, delayed response, and confusion during critical situations.

We bridge this gap by curating and verifying emergency hotline numbers, profiling trusted volunteer responders, mapping evacuation centers, and providing essential first-aid knowledge — all in one easy-to-navigate website.

Our Vision

A Makati City where every resident is informed, prepared, and empowered to act during emergencies. We envision a community where help is always just a click or a call away.

Core Values

  • Accessibility — emergency info available to everyone, anytime
  • Accuracy — only verified hotlines and validated providers
  • Community — built by residents, for residents
  • Preparedness — proactive education over reactive panic
  • Transparency — open-source information, no hidden agendas

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about our services and how the hub works.

What is LIFELINE Makati?
LIFELINE Makati is a community-based emergency services website that provides a centralized directory of verified hotline numbers, service provider profiles, first-aid guides, and evacuation center information for Makati City residents. It is an academic project built for ITS121L.
Are the emergency numbers verified and up to date?
Yes. All hotline numbers listed on the Services Directory page have been cross-referenced with official government agencies and publicly available records. However, as this is an academic project, users should always verify numbers through official channels during real emergencies.
How do I report a hazard or emergency in my area?
Navigate to the Contact & Inquiry page and fill out the hazard reporting form. Select "Report a Hazard" as the inquiry type, choose your barangay, set the urgency level, and describe the situation. You can also attach a photo for documentation.
How can I volunteer as a service provider?
Visit the Contact & Inquiry page and select "Volunteer Registration" as the inquiry type. Provide your name, contact details, and a brief description of your skills and experience. Our team will review your application and reach out within a few business days.
What is the interactive first aid image map?
The First Aid Image Map is an HTML <map> element overlaid on a first aid kit image. Each clickable area (using rect, circle, and poly shapes) highlights a different item in the kit — such as bandages, antiseptic wipes, scissors, pain relievers, a CPR mask, and an emergency blanket — with tooltips explaining their use.
Is this website for commercial use?
No. LIFELINE Makati is strictly an academic project developed for ITS121L — Web Systems and Technologies 1 Laboratory. It is not affiliated with any government office or commercial entity. All content is for educational and demonstrative purposes only.
